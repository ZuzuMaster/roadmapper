About Roadmapper

Roadmapper is a visual task and learning tool designed to help you plan, organize, and track your goals step-by-step using interactive node maps.

Key Features

  • Visual Roadmaps: Create grid-based maps for any project, study plan, or goal.
  • Node Connections: Link nodes together to map out prerequisites and dependencies.
  • Progress Tracking: Mark nodes as complete to watch your progress unfold with clear visual cues and celebration confetti!
  • Links: Attach useful external resources and references directly to your roadmap nodes.

How to Use

  1. Create a Roadmap: Log in and click the + button in your sidebar to spin up a new roadmap.
  2. Add Nodes: Open your roadmap's options menu and select Add node to build out your steps onto the grid.
  3. Connect Steps: Use the Connect tool to draw directional lines between dependent tasks.
  4. Track Your Progress: Click the checkmark on any node when finished. Optional branches let you take alternative paths without affecting your core completion score!
